Have More Questions?

What makes SSPU's B.Tech Defence Technology program unique?

SSPU's B.Tech in Defence Technology is built on a curriculum co-created with DRDO, defence industry leaders, and senior Armed Forces officers. It follows a 70:30 applied learning model with a dedicated Smart Centre of Excellence – Manufacturing Lab. The program is designed in direct alignment with Aatmanirbhar Bharat, Make in India – Defence, and India's Defence Industrial Corridors — making it one of the most mission-relevant defence engineering degrees in India.

Co-Created with DRDO 70:30 Applied Learning Aatmanirbhar Bharat

What technologies and domains does the program cover?

The program covers aerospace systems, defence electronics, cyber security & AI, and defence manufacturing — from propulsion and airborne platforms to embedded systems, radar, communications and ECM, robotics and autonomous systems, and weapons, vehicles, surveillance systems and indigenous production.

Aerospace Systems Defence Electronics Cyber & AI Defence Manufacturing

Where can graduates of this program work?

Graduates can pursue roles such as DRDO Scientist, Defence Systems Engineer, Aerospace Engineer, Embedded Systems Engineer, Cybersecurity Analyst — Defence, Defence Manufacturing Engineer, Robotics & Autonomous Systems Engineer, Radar & Communications Engineer, Weapons Systems Analyst and Defence R&D Project Manager across the defence and aerospace employer network, including DRDO, HAL, BEL, BEML and Tata Defence.

DRDO Scientist Defence Systems Engineer Aerospace Engineer

What are the eligibility and entrance requirements?

Indian Nationals: 10+2 (Physics & Maths) with min. 50% marks (45% for Reserved) and a valid JEE, MHT-CET, CUET, or SEET score. NRI / International Students: 10+2 (Physics & Maths) with min. 50% marks, AIU equivalence, and JEE or SEET qualification.
